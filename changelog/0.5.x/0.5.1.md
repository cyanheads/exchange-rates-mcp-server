# 0.5.1 — 2026-10-24

## Added

- **`fx_get_timeseries.summary`** reports the full requested interval's arithmetic mean, extrema, endpoints and percentage change before paging or staging; empty series omit it. The mean does not prescribe accounting policy. [#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/exchange-rates-mcp-server/issues/31)
- **Currency discovery** adds `as_of`, the latest served rate date, to `fx_list_currencies` and `fx://currencies`.

## Changed

- **Provenance** identifies `source: "Frankfurter v1"` and `rate_type: "Daily rate (Frankfurter v1)"` across responses, documentation and package metadata without inferring individual providers.
- **Bun runtime** `1.4.3 → 1.4.4` in package-manager metadata and container images.

## Fixed

- **FX precision** derives pair, conversion, bulk, resource and series rates locally from one unfiltered EUR snapshot or range without intermediate rounding. [#27](https://github.com/cyanheads/exchange-rates-mcp-server/issues/27)
- **Historical availability** validates against served data, including retired currencies, before inclusive clipping; each series observation requires both currency legs. Current discovery remains separate. [#28](https://github.com/cyanheads/exchange-rates-mcp-server/issues/28)
- **Explicit staging** honors `canvas_id` for short and empty ranges, preserves earlier tables with unique names and retains `rate DOUBLE`. Unknown or unavailable canvases fail explicitly; complete previews report `truncated: false`. [#29](https://github.com/cyanheads/exchange-rates-mcp-server/issues/29)
- **Input normalization** trims and uppercases currency values, advertises date patterns while retaining typed calendar errors and literal point-date `latest`, corrects required `end_date` recovery, and clamps positive integral SQL row limits above 10,000 with composed notices. [#30](https://github.com/cyanheads/exchange-rates-mcp-server/issues/30)
